    🎓 About Me

    I am a Ph.D. student with Prof. Xiao HUANG (since May 2025) at the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Prior to that, I obtained my B.E. in Energy and Power Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2020–2024).

    My research interests include Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), LLM Agents, and Tool Learning, with work published at ICLR and WWW. Currently, I focus on LLM agents, with a growing interest in embodied AI and spatial intelligence.
